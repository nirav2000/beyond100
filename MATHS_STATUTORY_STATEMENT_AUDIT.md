# Beyond100 Maths Statutory Statement Audit

## Scope and method

This is the statement-level audit of the England National Curriculum mathematics programmes of study for Years 1-6 and Key Stage 3.

Each statutory teaching statement is paraphrased (not copied as teaching content) and mapped to a canonical Beyond100 skill path. Non-statutory examples/guidance are used to refine decomposition but do not count as statutory statements.

Source of truth: Department for Education, National curriculum in England: mathematics programmes of study (current GOV.UK publication, last updated 28 September 2021).

Status:
- **MAPPED** - the revised catalogue has an adequate canonical home.
- **REFINE** - the broad home exists but needs an explicit atomic skill or stronger decomposition.
- **ADD** - a genuine catalogue item was missing and must be added.
- **HORIZON** - statutory KS3 content is mapped but may sit beyond Beyond100's current practical Year 7 horizon.

## Audit findings

### No missing statutory domains
Every Year 1-6 statutory statement now has a canonical home or an explicit refinement/addition action. Roman numerals is no longer an orphan.

### Explicit additions required
The pass found these items that were absent or too implicit in the catalogue:
1. metric-imperial approximate equivalence and miles/km conversion;
2. foundational position/turn language and some early measure decomposition;
3. number combinations for money and 2D-on-3D surface recognition as atomic skills;
4. area/perimeter independence;
5. operation-structure classification (additive vs multiplicative vs proportional);
6. linear/quadratic graph progression and y=mx+c;
7. standard form;
8. signed rational arithmetic;
9. calculator/technology interpretation;
10. rational/real/integer-set awareness;
11. geometric sequences;
12. ratio-to-linear-function structural link;
13. density as a compound measure;
14. triangle congruence criteria;
15. Pythagoras and trigonometric ratios;
16. sets/unions/intersections/Venn diagrams;
17. grouped-data distributions;
18. more explicit mathematical modelling/inference-limit skills.

### Progression rule
Because KS3 is statutory across Years 7-9, Beyond100 must not label all KS3 rows as Year 7. Stable skill IDs should carry a stage such as `early-ks3`, `ks3` or `later-ks3`, while the personalised engine may expose a skill earlier if prerequisites are secure.

### Source integrity
The audit deliberately distinguishes statutory requirements from DfE non-statutory examples and guidance. The latter can improve teaching and diagnostics but must not be presented as statutory requirements.
