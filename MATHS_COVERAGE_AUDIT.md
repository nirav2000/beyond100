# Maths Coverage Audit

This document is the audit ledger for the Beyond100 Maths catalogue. It is intentionally separate from the teaching content.

## Coverage sources

### Statutory
- England National Curriculum mathematics programmes of study: KS1 and KS2.
- England National Curriculum mathematics programme of study: KS3.

### Extension
- selective-school 11+/12+ mathematics and quantitative-reasoning material used by the learner;
- 1729 Maths School published admissions and educational philosophy;
- learner work and assessment evidence.

## Audit principle

A source statement is covered only if it maps to a stable canonical skill. A broad heading with no teachable skill underneath it does not count.

Status values:
- MAPPED - canonical home identified;
- PARTIAL - home exists but needs further decomposition;
- GAP - no adequate canonical home yet;
- EXTENSION - deliberately beyond statutory content.

## 1729 Maths School layer

1729's published admissions information says its assessment focuses on problem solving and mathematical exploration rather than knowledge beyond the National Curriculum, with oral activities centred on mathematical reasoning/problem solving. Its published educational material emphasises curiosity, persistence, uncertainty, underlying structure, connections, argument, abstraction and proof.

These become explicit evidence dimensions rather than a new accelerated syllabus.

## Required next audit passes

The domain-level statutory map above establishes that the revised catalogue has a home for the major curriculum requirements. It does **not** yet prove atomic completeness.

The next passes are:

1. Statement-level KS1/KS2 audit: every individual statutory bullet -> canonical skill ID.
2. Statement-level KS3 audit: every individual statutory bullet -> canonical skill ID and likely progression band.
3. 11+/12+ paper audit: classify every question from representative selective-school papers.
4. Sai evidence audit: map every existing Beyond100 assessment item and uploaded school-work error to a skill ID.
5. 1729 problem audit: map published/example problems and future problems to both content skills and mathematical-thinking tags.
6. Gap report: anything unclassifiable becomes a catalogue issue, never "Other".
7. Automated validator: fail catalogue QA if statutory source references have no canonical skill mapping.

## Consequence for mastery

Beyond100 should distinguish:

- **content mastery** - knows and can use the mathematics;
- **fluency** - accurate and appropriately efficient;
- **reasoning mastery** - can explain, connect, justify and transfer;
- **retention** - remains secure after delay;
- **mathematical-thinking development** - curiosity, structure, persistence, conjecture, proof, abstraction and related 1729-aligned behaviours.

A learner can therefore be ahead in content but still need richer reasoning, or have excellent mathematical thinking while needing a specific knowledge gap repaired.
